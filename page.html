How speed is estimated: points on each detected vehicle are followed between two frames (0.2 s apart on live video, ~0.4 s apart in TranStar bursts), and the vehicle's own size is the ruler (typical car 4.5 m long, 1.8 m wide). No per-camera calibration, so treat speeds as rough (±30% or worse). At night, long exposures smear moving cars into longer streaks, so speeds read low. TranStar's burst frame gap is only known to ±0.1 s (another ±25%). Cameras that post single stills can't show speed.
